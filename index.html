Color palettes that fit what you're making.

Describe your project, pick a mood, or spin the wheel. Chromia builds the palette, checks it for contrast and color blindness, and gives you the code.

Generator

Lock the colors you like, then generate the rest. Switch formats, pick a harmony, or shift the tone with presets and sliders.

Saved palettes

Moods and stories

Start from a feeling. Each palette comes with the scene it was built around.

Color wheel

Drag on the wheel to set the base hue and saturation. The current harmony mode shapes the rest of the palette.

Use the pointer or arrow keys.

Tints and shades

Nine steps from near-white to near-black for every color in the palette. Click any step to copy it.

Design system

Chromia turns your palette into 12 named roles for light and dark themes, adjusts colors until text passes WCAG AA, checks status colors for color blindness, and exports code you can use.
